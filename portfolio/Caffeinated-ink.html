My Journey

Tap any icon for a quick look into process and outcomes

1

Project Direction

Set clear business and design priorities upfront,
defining what to solve, what to ignore, and what
success should look like before opening Figma.

2

Market Research

Analysed local and global coffee brands
to uncover authenticity signals, generic
conventions, and key points of differentiation.

3

Brand Identity

Designed a brand that doesn’t decorate the
space, but amplifies it—translating tattoo
culture and specialty coffee into a single,
credible voice.

4

IA Structure

Built an intent-driven information architecture
enabling coffee and tattoo to coexist, later
extended with an admin layers for operations.

5

Wireframing

Used wireframes to lock decisions on hierarchy,
density, and tone early, treating layout
and copy as one system, not separate layers.

6

UI System

Built a UI system that actively guides
behavior where components, states,
and interactions reduce friction instead
of adding polish.

7

Prototyping

Used the prototype to stress-test the product,
expose operational gaps, and expand the
system beyond the initial design scope.

8

User Validation

Validated assumptions through task-based
testing, focusing on real actions rather
than opinions or aesthetic feedback.

9

AI Usage

Used AI to accelerate thinking and
iteration, not to outsource decisions or
dilute design accountability.
